## Editorial controls
1. Each query family gets a reader-serving article and a single stable canonical URL.
2. Internal links describe the destination in natural English, rather than mechanically repeating keywords.
3. Source notes distinguish documented bibliographic facts from literary interpretation.
4. Full original Spanish poem remains unchanged; English summaries are not misrepresented as translations.
5. Published English home can be indexable because the collection now has substantive English pages; indexing does not guarantee search appearance.
6. Use five dormant ad reservations per article: one immediately after opening BODY via Base; three distributed through editorial sections; one following the article's sources. Reservations are hidden and contain no ad scripts.
